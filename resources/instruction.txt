HOMEPAGE PROMPT – ST. TAMMANY PARISH FENCING PROS
=================================================

GOAL
Create a professional, high-converting homepage for a local fencing company called **St. Tammany Parish Fencing Pros**.  
The design and structure should be clean, modern, and easily reusable for other fencing companies (including New Orleans Fencing Pros).

Design Style: Industrial Material Design 3 with controlled 3D scroll effects  
(subtle hero parallax, light card lift/tilt on scroll, and soft depth on process steps).

COLOR PALETTE (exact – do not change)
- Primary Dark: #1A1A1A (Deep Charcoal)
- Accent: #2E7D32 (Forest Green)
- Background Light: #F8F7F4 (Warm Off-White)
- Background Soft: #E8EDE8 (Soft Sage Gray)
- Secondary Text: #5C5A55 (Warm Medium Gray)
- White: #FFFFFF
- Border / Divider: #D6D3CD

INSPIRATION SITES
- https://texasfencebuilders.net/
- https://houstonfence.com/
- https://carolinafencecompany.com/

Also take visual and layout inspiration from: Dribbble, Mobbin, Behance, Awwwards, Godly, Collect UI, Refero, Land-book, Lapa Ninja, SiteInspire, Page Flows, One Page Love, Screenlane, and Muzli.

COMPANY
Name: St. Tammany Parish Fencing Pros  
Location: St. Tammany Parish, Louisiana (Northshore – Covington, Mandeville, islidell, and surrounding areas)  
Tone: Professional, reliable, local, and high-quality

HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE

1. Sticky Header
- Dark background (#1A1A1A)
- Company logo + name
- Navigation: Services, Projects, Areas, About, Contact
- Phone number
- “Get Free Estimate” button in Forest Green (#2E7D32)

2. Hero Section
- Strong full-width image of a finished, high-quality fence
- Headline: “Quality Fencing Built for Louisiana Homes”
- Subheadline: “Wood, Vinyl, Iron & Privacy Fences Installed Across St. Tammany Parish”
- Two buttons: “Get Free Estimate” and “Call Now”
- Trust line under buttons: Locally Owned • Licensed & Insured • Free On-Site Estimates

3. Trust Bar
Display key trust points in a clean horizontal strip:
- Licensed & Insured
- 15+ Years Experience
- 1,000+ Fences Installed
- Workmanship Warranty
- Free Estimates

4. Services Section
Heading: Fencing Services We Offer

Show as clean cards with subtle scroll depth:
- Wood Fencing
- Vinyl Fencing
- Ornamental Iron & Aluminum
- Privacy Fencing
- Commercial Fencing
- Custom Gates
- Fence Repair
- Fence Replacement

Add a “View All Services” button at the bottom.

5. Process Section
Heading: How We Work
Show a clear 4-step process:
1. Free On-Site Estimate
2. Material Selection & Design
3. Professional Installation
4. Final Walkthrough & Cleanup

6. Projects Section
Heading: Recent Fence Projects  
Clean photo grid (3 or 4 columns).  
Button: “View Full Gallery”

7. Service Areas
Heading: Areas We Serve  
Text: Serving St. Tammany Parish and nearby communities including Covington, Mandeville, Slidell, Madisonville, Abita Springs, Lacombe, and Pearl River.  
Include a simple map.  
Button: “See All Areas”

8. Free Estimate Form (Important)
Heading: Request Your Free Fence Estimate  
Intro text: “Fill out the form below and we’ll get back to you with pricing.”

Form fields:
- Full Name
- Phone Number
- Email
- Property Address
- Fence Material (Wood, Vinyl, Iron, Aluminum, Privacy, Other)
- Approximate Linear Feet
- Fence Height (4ft, 5ft, 6ft, 8ft, Custom)
- Project Type (New Fence, Replacement, Repair, Gate)
- Additional Details
- Submit button: “Get My Free Estimate” (Forest Green #2E7D32)

Place the phone number prominently next to the form.

9. Footer
Simple and clean with company name, phone, and basic links.

ADDITIONAL INSTRUCTIONS
- Keep the content professional and natural for a fencing company.
- Make the Free Estimate form the main conversion focus.
- Use subtle 3D scroll effects only.
- Mobile-first and high contrast.
- Design should feel trustworthy and local.

Build a clean, modern homepage that helps visitors quickly understand the services and easily request a free fencing estimate.
